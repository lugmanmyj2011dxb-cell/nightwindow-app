Privacy Policy — Sleep Window

Last updated: 2026-11-05 · Applies to Sleep Window (眠窗) published by Sleep Window Studio
Sleep Window is a sleep-focused ambient sound and visual app. We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

1. Information we handle

  • Account data (optional): if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive an account identifier and email address through Firebase Authentication, used only to keep your membership status across devices.
  • Purchase data: subscription and in-app purchase receipts are processed by RevenueCat and the Google Play / App Store billing systems so we can verify your entitlement. We never receive your full payment card details.
  • Technical data: anonymous app version and platform information may be logged to diagnose crashes and delivery problems.
  • What we do NOT collect: precise location, contacts, calendar, photos, microphone audio recordings, advertising identifiers, or health records.

2. How we use data

  • To restore and maintain your membership entitlement.
  • To deliver remote media (scene videos) and to keep the app stable.
  • To answer support requests you send us.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app: Google Firebase (authentication and content hosting), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Google Play / Apple App Store (billing). We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Retention and deletion

Account identifiers are kept while your account exists. To delete your account and associated data, email us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Access to backend services is limited to what is required for the features above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.
