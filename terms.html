Night Window's live-mixed sounds, switchable scenes and saved mixes/logs are closer to a digital service, so we take the approach more favourable to you:

If you ask to withdraw (refund) within 14 days of purchase, we will in principle process it even if you have started using the service. Under Article 14(3) of the Directive we may deduct a proportionate amount for the service you actually used and refund the rest — we will not simply refuse, and the deduction will be reasonable, not punitive. If you have not used any members-only content at all, you will receive a full refund.
